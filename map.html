Related resource
Tabula Peutingeriana
The 13th-century parchment copy of the Roman road map stretching from Britain to India — the primary source behind OmnesViae's route data.
Talbert, Rome's World ↗ The canonical scholarly edition. OmnesViae data is based on Talbert's reconstructions. Piggin Digital Plot SVG ↗ Jean-Baptiste Piggin's complete digital vector rendering (CC BY-SA) — color-coded Italian routes, 3,300+ places. Open raw SVG for full horizontal scroll. tabula-peutingeriana.de ↗ High-resolution facsimile and segment viewer of the Vienna parchment scroll (ÖNB Cod. 324). Wikimedia Commons ↗ Public domain segment scans for direct download and scholarly reuse.
Original: Vienna, Österreichische Nationalbibliothek, Cod. Vindob. 324. Parchment scroll, 13th c. CE copy of 4th c. original. 6.82 m × 0.34 m. England → Sri Lanka.
OmnesViae routes the Roman cursus publicus using distances from the Tabula Peutingeriana and Antonine Itinerary. Enter two cities to find the shortest Roman road path. Developed by René Voorburg. The sidebar links to primary facsimiles and the Piggin SVG for direct study of the map itself.
